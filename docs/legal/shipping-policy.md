# Shipping policy — draft

**Status:** ready for Steve's review. Every figure comes from Store Operating Standards §8 or the
project brief §13. No invented numbers.
**Destination:** Shopify → Settings → Policies → Shipping policy.
**Voice:** brand-compliant — no em-dashes, no exclamation marks, plain and direct.

**Two flags before you paste it:**

1. **"Within one to three business days"** assumes a Tampa 3PL that has not been selected. It is the
   brief's own figure, but it is the one line here that a vendor could make untrue. If you would rather
   not commit before signing, say *"as soon as it is packed, usually within a few business days"* and
   tighten it once the 3PL is chosen.
2. **Transit estimates** are the carrier's, not ours, and the wording below says so. That distinction
   matters: an estimate you present as a promise becomes one.

---

## Shipping

**Where we ship**

We ship within the United States only, including all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and US
territories. We do not ship internationally at this time.

**What shipping costs**

| Order | Shipping |
|---|---|
| Roccia subscription shipments | Free, always, with no minimum |
| One-time orders of $55 or more | Free |
| One-time orders under $55 | $8.50 flat |

Free shipping on Roccia is part of your subscriber benefit and applies to every recurring shipment.

**Who carries it**

Orders under one pound ship USPS Ground Advantage. Orders of one pound or more ship UPS Ground. We do
not offer expedited shipping at this time, and we do not use FedEx.

**When it arrives**

We pack and hand your order to the carrier within one to three business days. After that, carrier
transit times from our Tampa, Florida warehouse are typically:

- East and Southeast: 2 to 3 business days
- Midwest and Mountain: 3 to 4 business days
- West Coast: 4 to 5 business days

These are the carrier's estimates rather than guarantees. Weather, holidays, and carrier delays are
outside our control.

**How your coffee is packed**

Your coffee ships in the roaster's own sealed bag, exactly as it left the roastery. We do not
repackage, re-bag, or relabel it. Orders of one or two bags ship in a recyclable Kraft mailer; three
or more bags, or any 1 kg bag, ship in a box.

Every package includes a packing slip. **No document inside any package shows a price.** Your receipt
is the order confirmation we email you.

**Tracking**

You will receive a shipping confirmation with tracking as soon as your order leaves the warehouse.

**If something arrives damaged, or does not arrive**

Tell us and we will send a replacement the same day. You do not need to return the original, and you
do not need to send photographs unless you want to. This applies to damage in transit and to packages
that a carrier marks delivered but you never received.

**Freshness in transit**

We air-freight our coffee from Italy rather than shipping it by sea, and we do not sell coffee past
our freshness window. Every bag shows its roast date. Domestic transit is a matter of days, not weeks,
and your coffee is sealed for all of it.

**Questions**

Email us at support@cremaitalia.com.

## Notes for the reviewer, not for the page

- **The damage clause is deliberately generous and deliberately cheap.** Standard §8 already commits
  to same-day replacement with the customer keeping the original, tracked as a P&L line. Saying so
  plainly converts a cost you have already accepted into a reason to trust you.
- **"No document inside any package shows a price"** is Standard §8.1, and it is worth stating on a
  customer-facing page rather than leaving as an internal rule. It is the sentence that makes every
  order giftable without a flag.
- **`support@cremaitalia.com` exists as of 2026-08-22.** Confirm it is monitored before this page
  goes live, and decide alias-vs-Group first — an address on a policy page is a promise to answer.
- **Do not add a returns address here.** See `README.md`: the recommendation is that coffee is never
  returned physically, which is also why this policy needs no 3PL decision.
