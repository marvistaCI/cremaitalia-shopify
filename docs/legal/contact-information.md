## Decisions this needs before it goes live

**1. Which address do you publish?** The one above is the LLC's registered mailing address, which is
a residential address in Lutz. Everything already in the record uses it — the brief §2, the FDA US
Agent filing, and the roaster-facing commercial invoice template.

Publishing a home address on a public policy page is a real and permanent decision. The alternatives:
a commercial mail-receiving agency box, the Tampa 3PL's address once selected, or the registered
agent's address. **This is worth deciding deliberately rather than inheriting from the brief**, and it
is the only reason this page is not already done.

Note the FDA filing is a separate matter — that address is on file with a regulator and does not have
to be the one on the website.

**2. Which email do you publish, and is it monitored?** `info@` and `support@` both exist as of
2026-08-22, and both currently deliver into one inbox as Workspace aliases. Publishing two addresses
implies two destinations and two response paths. Either commit to that, or publish one.

Related, and still open in the tracker: **alias versus Google Group for `support@`**. A published
support address that reaches exactly one person does not survive that person being on a plane.

**3. Do you publish a phone number?** Dialpad exists and the FDA holds `+1-813-376-4821` as the US
Agent contact. The storefront currently publishes no number anywhere. A phone number on the contact
record raises trust measurably for a new merchant, and it commits you to answering it. Your call, and
it is the same decision already sitting in the tracker as *"Does the site publish a phone number?"*
