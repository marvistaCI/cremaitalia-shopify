## One recommendation before you paste

**Do not put "pending legal review" on the public pages.** Track it in the tracker and in this repo
instead.

A visible *"these policies are unreviewed"* banner on a public page is worse than no banner for
exactly the audience it reaches: it tells a customer not to rely on the thing they are reading, and it
tells a payment processor you know the pages are incomplete. Nothing is being concealed by leaving it
off — every clause below describes what the business actually does, which is the real standard. "Drafted
by counsel" is not a claim anyone makes on a policy page, so omitting the caveat asserts nothing false.

**The caveat belongs where it changes behaviour:** the Decisions sheet, so nobody forgets, and the
interim choices flagged in §5 below.

# 2 · SHIPPING POLICY

```
Where we ship

We ship within the United States only, including all 50 states, the District of
Columbia, and US territories. We do not ship internationally at this time.

What shipping costs

Roccia subscription shipments ship free, always, with no minimum. One-time orders of
$55 or more ship free. One-time orders under $55 carry a flat $8.50 shipping charge.

Free shipping on Roccia is part of your subscriber benefit and applies to every
recurring shipment.

Who carries it

Orders under one pound ship USPS Ground Advantage. Orders of one pound or more ship
UPS Ground. We do not offer expedited shipping at this time, and we do not use FedEx.

When it arrives

We pack your order and hand it to the carrier within one to three business days.
After that, carrier transit times from our Tampa, Florida warehouse are typically
2 to 3 business days to the East and Southeast, 3 to 4 to the Midwest and Mountain
states, and 4 to 5 to the West Coast.

These are the carrier's estimates rather than guarantees. Weather, holidays, and
carrier delays are outside our control.

How your coffee is packed

Your coffee ships in the roaster's own sealed bag, exactly as it left the roastery.
We do not repackage, re-bag, or relabel it. Orders of one or two bags ship in a
recyclable Kraft mailer; three or more bags, or any 1 kg bag, ship in a box.

Every package includes a packing slip. No document inside any package shows a price.
Your receipt is the order confirmation we email you.

Tracking

You will receive a shipping confirmation with tracking as soon as your order leaves
the warehouse.

If something arrives damaged, or does not arrive

Tell us and we will send a replacement the same day. You do not need to return the
original. This applies to damage in transit and to packages a carrier marks delivered
that you never received.

Address accuracy

Please check your shipping address at checkout. We ship to the address provided and
cannot guarantee redirection once an order has shipped.

Questions

Email support@cremaitalia.com.
```

# 3 · RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

```
Our promise on Roccia, Sorpresa and Selezione

Love your first bag, or we will send you a different one, free. If a coffee is not
right for you, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we will send a replacement from
a different roaster or a different coffee at no charge. This covers one replacement
per order.

Offerta is sold as-is

Offerta coffee is older stock at an honest markdown, and you see its freshness
information before you buy. We stand behind genuine defects, including a bag that
arrives damaged or faulty. We do not exchange or refund Offerta coffee because you
would have preferred it fresher. That is what the discount reflects.

Damaged or lost in transit

If your order arrives damaged, or does not arrive at all, tell us and we will send a
replacement the same day. Keep the original. We do not need it back.

Why we do not ask for coffee back

Roasted coffee is a perishable food. Once a bag has left our warehouse we cannot
resell it, so asking you to pay to return something we would then discard helps
nobody. We replace it or refund it instead.

Equipment and merchandise

Bottega equipment and merchandise are different, because they are not perishable.
Unopened items in original condition can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a
refund. Contact us first and we will send return instructions.

Subscriptions

You can skip, swap, pause, or cancel a Roccia subscription at any time from your
account. Cancelling stops future shipments immediately. If you cancel after a cycle
has been billed but before it ships, we refund that charge in full.

How to ask

Email support@cremaitalia.com with your order number. For damage, a photograph helps
but is not required. We aim to resolve every request within a few business days.
```

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your
choices.

What we collect

Contact and order information you give us: name, email, shipping and billing address,
optional phone number, and order history.

Preference data: your answers to our taste-profile quiz, used to suggest coffees you
are likely to enjoy. This is preference data, not sensitive personal information.

Reviews you choose to write, which are linked to your customer account so that we can

Browsing and device data collected automatically through cookies and similar

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store birthdates, government identification numbers, or other

How we use it

To fulfil and ship your order. To manage your Roccia subscription, including billing
